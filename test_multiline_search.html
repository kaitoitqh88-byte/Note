Test Instructions
  1. Open search.php in your browser
  2. Look for the new toggle button labeled "Nhiều Domain" in the top-right of the search box
  3. Click the toggle button to switch to multi-line mode
  4. Enter multiple domains in the textarea using different formats:
    • Comma-separated: domain1.com, domain2.org
    • Line-separated: Each domain on a new line
    • Mixed separators: domain1.com; domain2.org\ndomain3.net
  5. Press Ctrl+Enter or wait for auto-search to trigger the search
  6. Verify the enhanced results display shows search statistics
