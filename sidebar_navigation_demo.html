How to Use

Desktop (1200px+)

Sidebar automatically opens and stays visible. Main content adjusts with margin-left. Click menu items to navigate between pages.

Tablet (769px - 1199px)

Sidebar hidden by default. Click the hamburger menu button (☰) in the top-left to open. Click anywhere outside the sidebar or press Escape to close.

Mobile (768px and below)

Full-width overlay sidebar. Automatically closes when selecting menu items. Swipe gestures supported for closing.
